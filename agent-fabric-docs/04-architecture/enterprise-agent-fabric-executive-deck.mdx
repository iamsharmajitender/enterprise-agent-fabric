# Enterprise Agent Fabric

> **One governed front door for enterprise AI**

**Status:** Draft · **Date:** 2 September 2026

[Full decision paper](./enterprise-agent-fabric-pitch.mdx) · [Technical design](./enterprise-agent-fabric-architecture.mdx)

---

## What it is

The **Enterprise Agent Fabric** is a shared operating layer for enterprise AI — not one giant agent, but the infrastructure between users and domain-owned capabilities.

- **One front door** — employees, customers, applications, and jobs enter in one place
- **Many specialised capabilities** — Finance, HR, Legal, Claims, Payments, and more stay domain-owned
- **One control model** — identity, entitlement, policy, audit, monitoring, and evaluation applied consistently

> Scale AI as a **governed portfolio**, not a collection of disconnected applications.

---

## The problem

AI capabilities are emerging independently across the enterprise.

| Audience | Examples |
| --- | --- |
| **Employee** | Finance, HR, Legal, Technology assistants |
| **Customer** | Service AI, Claims AI |
| **Partner** | Payments AI, KYC AI |

Every team rebuilds its own front door, auth, routing, audit, and ops.

The organisation loses a single answer to three questions:

- **Who** may use this capability?
- **What** is it allowed to do?
- **Why** was it selected, and what did it do?

## Why now

AI is moving from **answering questions** to **performing work and taking actions**.

- Deeper integration with enterprise data, customers, money, and operations
- Greater need for approval, audit, and accountability
- Higher risk when every team invents its own controls

Without a common operating model, each new capability adds duplication, inconsistency, and governance overhead.

> The question shifts from *“how do we build another AI app?”* to *“how do we onboard another governed capability?”*

---

## What we propose

A **shared operating layer** between users and specialised AI.

| The Fabric handles | Domains keep |
| --- | --- |
| **Who** may act — identity and entitlement | Workflows and business logic |
| **Which** capability responds — routing and policy | Domain knowledge and expertise |
| **How** work runs safely — audit, approval, monitoring, ops | Models, tools, and outcomes |

## What you get

- **One AI experience** — users ask the enterprise, not a specific assistant
- **Faster onboarding** — register a governed capability, not another platform stack
- **Consistent controls** — entitlement, policy, kill switch, and least-privilege once for all
- **Clear accountability** — one evidence model across the portfolio
- **Operational visibility** — shared health and failure view across the platform
- **Safer change** — routing and behaviour gated before production
- **Failure isolation** — one capability failing does not take down enterprise AI
- **Domain autonomy** — business teams keep speed; the platform owns the common path

> **Outcome:** scale AI adoption without scaling fragmentation and risk at the same rate.

---

## How it works

Every request follows the same governed path:

| Step | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| **One experience** | Enter through the enterprise front door |
| **Governed decision** | Understand intent, select capability, verify entitlement and policy |
| **Specialised capability** | Domain AI runs with approved data and tools |
| **Controlled action** | High-risk steps gated by policy, approval, or human handoff |
| **Auditable outcome** | Record what was decided, executed, and approved |

Chat and automated jobs share the same contract. Ingress paths scale independently.

---

## What it is not

| Not this | Instead |
| --- | --- |
| One giant enterprise agent | An operating layer around specialised capabilities |
| A replacement for domain AI teams | Domains keep ownership of use cases and outcomes |
| One model or runtime for everything | A common contract; implementations stay flexible |
| Central ownership of every use case | Centralised controls, decentralised capability |

**Centralise:** identity, routing, policy, audit, observability, evaluation, reliability.

**Decentralise:** business logic, workflows, knowledge, specialised quality.

---

## The ask

Endorse the **Enterprise Agent Fabric** as the enterprise pattern for governed AI.

Support **V1** to:

- Harden the platform for production
- Onboard priority capabilities across domains
- Establish enterprise standards for controls, audit, and evaluation

> **Decision:** scale AI as a governed capability portfolio — or keep scaling disconnected AI applications independently.
