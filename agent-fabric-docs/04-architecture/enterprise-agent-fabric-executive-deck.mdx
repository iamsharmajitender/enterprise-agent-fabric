# Enterprise Agent Fabric

## Executive Brief

### One governed front door for enterprise AI

**2 September 2026**

[Full decision paper](./enterprise-agent-fabric-pitch.mdx) · [Technical design](./enterprise-agent-fabric-architecture.mdx)

---

## The opportunity

AI is growing quickly across the organisation. Teams are building capabilities for **employees, customers, partners, and operations** — good for innovation, but it creates a structural problem.

**Today:** many AI apps → many approaches → many controls.

Each team may independently build experiences, access controls, routing, audit, monitoring, and governance. We risk **solving the same problem many times** and operating AI differently everywhere.

The organisation loses one answer to: **who** may use a capability, **what** it may do, and **why** it was selected and what it did.

## Our answer

**Enterprise Agent Fabric** — a shared enterprise layer between users and AI capabilities.

**One front door** → **one set of enterprise controls** → **many specialised AI capabilities**

Users do not need to know which capability sits behind the experience. Domains still own their AI; the Fabric provides the **common enterprise path**.

---

## What becomes common

- **Who can use it** — identity, entitlement, least-privilege access
- **Where the request goes** — routing and policy select the right capability
- **What it can do** — controls, approvals, and policy govern actions
- **What happened** — common audit, monitoring, and operational visibility
- **Is it safe to release** — evaluation and governance before production

---

## What stays with the domains

The Fabric does **not** centralise everything. Domains keep:

- Business logic, workflows, and knowledge
- Models, tools, and business outcomes

> **Centralise the controls. Decentralise the intelligence.**

---

## The value

- **Faster** — onboard a capability without rebuilding platform foundations
- **Safer** — consistent enterprise controls across the portfolio
- **More accountable** — who asked, what was selected, what happened, what was approved
- **Easier to scale** — add AI without adding the same platform complexity each time

---

## The shift

| From | To |
| --- | --- |
| Every team builds, controls, and operates independently | **Enterprise Fabric** — common controls and experience |
| Repeated platform work per capability | **Specialised domain AI** — independent and domain-owned |

---

## What V1 enables

1. Harden the platform for production
2. Onboard priority AI capabilities
3. Establish common enterprise standards
4. Prove the model through real business use cases
5. Create a repeatable pattern for future AI

---

## The decision

**Do we continue scaling AI applications independently?**

**Or do we establish one enterprise operating model for AI?**

**Recommendation:** proceed with **Enterprise Agent Fabric V1**.

Not one giant enterprise agent — **one governed way of operating many AI capabilities.**

---

> **One governed front door. Many specialised capabilities. One enterprise operating model.**
