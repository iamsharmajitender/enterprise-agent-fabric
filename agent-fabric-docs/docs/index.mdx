## What the platform is

The Fabric is the shared front door and control plane for **acting** AI — money, customer data, regulated process. Chatbots that only answer can live on their own. Anything that *does* something needs the same enterprise answers every time:

1. Who was allowed to use this?
2. What was it permitted to do?
3. Why this agent, and what did it actually do?

Those answers come from one place. Specialist agents stay in the domains that know the work.

## How a request travels

1. **Front Door** authenticates the caller and is the only public ingress.
2. **Agent Data Plane** classifies the turn (or entitles a named job) and never starts Runtime.
3. **Front Door** freezes the route version, then starts **Agent Runtime**.
4. **Runtime** hydrates the pinned capabilities and runs the agent.
5. **Audit** and **observability** record the same `correlation_id` so you can prove what happened.
